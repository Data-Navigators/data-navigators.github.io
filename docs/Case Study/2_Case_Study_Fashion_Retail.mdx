# Case Study 2: Fashion Retail Inventory and Profitability

## Business context

A fashion merchant operates wholesale and retail stores across multiple regions. The business has collected sales and purchase data over several years and wants to improve inventory decisions and store profitability.

Dataset: [Fashion Retail India on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ankitabhatia10/fashion-retail-india/data)

For practical purposes, assume that inventory on the first purchase date available in `purchase_data.csv` was zero.

## Your consulting assignment

Choose a stakeholder decision related to inventory, sales growth, store performance, or product profitability. Create your own problem statement and define what evidence the stakeholder needs before acting.

Useful investigation directions include best-selling products, current-month growth, inventory on hand, purchase planning, store profit margins, and products that reduce profitability over a recent period. These are prompts for scoping, not a fixed question list.

## Expected deliverable

Create a concise analysis covering data understanding, inventory or sales calculations, assumptions, findings, and a recommendation. Consider whether a product should be continued, discontinued, repriced, promoted, or managed differently. Explain the risks of the recommendation and identify the next data point the business should collect.
