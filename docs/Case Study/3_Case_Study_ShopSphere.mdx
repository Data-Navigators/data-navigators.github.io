# Case Study 3: ShopSphere E-Commerce Behavior

## Business context

A mid-sized e-commerce platform sells consumer goods from electronics to home decor. The business is growing, but it needs a clearer understanding of customer behavior, product demand, discounts, reviews, returns, and payment choices.

Dataset: [Synthetic ShopSphere E-Commerce Dataset on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nbazzaneze/synthetic-dataset/data)

## Your consulting assignment

Choose a stakeholder decision related to customer engagement, category growth, seasonal demand, discounts, returns, payment methods, or product and supplier quality. Create your own problem statement and explain which tables and measures can support it.

Useful investigation directions include products purchased together, seasonal category demand, recurring-customer spending, fast- and slow-moving categories, discount and return patterns, payment-method behavior, and review or supplier patterns. These directions are deliberately open so that you can choose a focused business problem.

## Expected deliverable

Create a concise consultant-style analysis with a defined stakeholder, data-quality review, metric definitions, evidence, recommendation, and limitations. If you study association between discounts, payment, returns, ratings, or reviews, state clearly what the data can and cannot establish about causation.
